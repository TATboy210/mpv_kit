  296: libplacebo has no Metal `pl_gpu`, and its backends are Vulkan, OpenGL and D3D11 ([libplacebo README](https://github.com/haasn/libplacebo)). The OpenGL plus IOSurface route (IINA's historical path) runs on Apple's deprecated GL-over-Metal layer and only gets vo_gpu (Pitfall 7). mpv's macOS GL has resize crashes in the GL driver ([mpv#10406](https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv/issues/10406)) and resize lag ([mpv#14173](https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv/issues/14173)). media_kit's macOS output freezes during window resize ([media-kit#1176](https://github.com/media-kit/media-kit/issues/1176)).
