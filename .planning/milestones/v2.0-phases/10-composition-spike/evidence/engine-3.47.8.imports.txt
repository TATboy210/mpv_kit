## What this means for fence / device-ownership work

`ANGLE EGL/GLES: compiled into this PE`

Concretely: ANGLE's EGL/GLES implementation is compiled into this PE. There is
no side-by-side `libEGL.dll` / `libGLESv2.dll` to swap, inspect, or replace.
Therefore the D3D11 interop synchronization semantics (fence, keyed mutex,
device ownership) can only be explained from this PE plus ANGLE's public
source; they cannot be isolated by "change one DLL and observe the
difference". The workload implication is that the fence question is answered
by decompiling the ANGLE paths inside `flutter_windows.dll` and correlating
them with public ANGLE source, not by version-swapping an ANGLE binary.

This settles the linkage question definitively (it was an inference in
`10-RESEARCH.md` before the 3.47.8 delta). Fence and device-ownership
semantics remain analysis work in Task 2 and Phase 11.
