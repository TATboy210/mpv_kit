## Consistency note

These counts are identical to the counts measured against the 3.47.6 DLL in
`10-RESEARCH.md`. The earlier "exactly once" expectation was a measurement
error, not a version difference: the additional hits are distinct literals
(`Using the Impeller rendering backend (OpenGLESSDF)`, `(OpenGLES)`,
`(VulkanSDF)`, `(Vulkan)`), and the two `Could not create external texture`
messages correspond to the Skia and Impeller wrap-failure sites.

Consequence for downstream verification: a gate that asserts `== 1` for
`Could not create external texture` or `Using the Impeller rendering backend`
fails against the real binary. Use the per-anchor counts above.
