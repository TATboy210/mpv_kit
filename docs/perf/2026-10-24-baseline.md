## Decode micro-benchmark (200k iterations, JIT, after 20k warm-up)

| Shape | ns/op | ops/s |
|-------|-------|-------|
| `decode-double` (time-pos shape, double node) | **169–175** | ~5.7–5.9 M |
| `decode-map` (2-entry track-list-like map) | **1768–1960** | ~0.51–0.57 M |

Run-to-run noise ≈ ±5%. The double shape double-decodes today (`_prop` called twice per event), so roughly half of its cost is eliminable.

| Metric | Value |

Metric caveat: `vo-delayed-frame-count` is a display-sync-mode metric; the Player default is `video-sync=audio`, so the backlog gates are the drop counts + `total-avsync-change` + `avsync`.

## Findings feeding 04-02

- `decode-double` ≈ 170 ns/op with a confirmed double `_prop` call (events.dart:28-33/54-59) — expect ≈50% reduction.
- The `time-pos` listener (player.dart:122-134) allocates 3 Durations + 1 DateTime per event (60/s at 60 fps), all before the 100 ms throttle decides.
